OUR PHILOSOPHY

Sunlight Filtering Through Trees

Komorebi (木漏れ日) is the Japanese word for sunlight filtering through leaves. We created our cafe around this single feeling: a warm, gentle pause in the middle of a busy day.

Whether you are stopping by for a quiet morning matcha, catching up with an old friend, or looking for a peaceful neighborhood spot in West Village, our doors are open to welcome you.
